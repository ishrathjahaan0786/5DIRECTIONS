# FIVE DIRECTIONS — DESIGN SYSTEM

## Brand & Product Identity
- **Brand Name:** 5Directions
- **Logo Asset:** Exact user-provided logo ({{DATA:IMAGE:IMAGE_2}}), displayed on cream/white surfaces with adequate clear space.
- **Tagline:** One disruption. Smarter alternatives. A resilient supply chain.
- **Visual Direction:** Bright, playful, interactive, optimistic, enterprise-grade supply-chain resilience platform. Crisp typography, generous whitespace, industrial imagery, and fluid visual hierarchy.

## Fiery Ocean Color System (Exact Hex)
- **Warm Cream:** `#FDF0D5` (Primary background, light surface warmth, hero accents)
- **Deep Navy:** `#003049` (Primary typography, dark structures, borders, navigational chrome)
- **Ocean Blue:** `#669BBC` (Secondary interactive elements, links, charts, diagrams, badges)
- **Crimson Red:** `#C1121F` (Primary Call To Action, signature highlights, disruption alerts)
- **Deep Burgundy:** `#780000` (Restrained brand accents, critical warnings, badges)
- **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` (Card surfaces, data tables, form inputs, modals for pristine readability)
- **Neutral Tint/Borders:** `#EADBC2` and `#E2E8F0` for subtle dividers.

## Typography
- **Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- **Headings:** Deep Navy (`#003049`), font-bold / font-extrabold with tight letter-spacing (`tracking-tight`)
- **Body Text:** Slate / Navy (`#003049` / `#1E3A5F`), high legibility and contrast
- **Microcopy & Captions:** Medium navy slate (`#4B6B88`)

## Elevation & Radii
- **Border Radius:** `rounded-2xl` for cards and panels, `rounded-xl` for buttons/inputs, `rounded-full` for badges
- **Shadows:** Soft natural elevation `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(0, 48, 73, 0.08)`

## Component Standards
- **Primary Action (CTA):** `#C1121F` background, white text, hover lift and shadow
- **Secondary Action:** `#003049` text on `#FDF0D5` cream or outline, hover subtle fill
- **Accent Badge / Tag:** `#669BBC` at 15% tint with `#003049` or `#004b73` text
- **Multi-Supplier Allocation:** Segmented progress meters with interactive tooltips, percentage indicators, and live feasibility calculations.
